Can you thaw a turkey in cold water overnight?

No. Cold water only works while you are awake to change it. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. An overnight soak breaks both rules at once.

Why cold water stops working overnight

Why cold water stops working overnight

Cold tap water warms toward room temperature, and the bird's outside warms with it. USDA calls 40 F to 140 F the danger zone, where bacteria grow fast. The changes are the method: fresh cold water holds the bird cold while it thaws. Nobody changes the water at 3 in the morning.

The same method, done in daylight

Seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag. Submerge it in cold tap water. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws.

Use the fridge overnight

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your microwave manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.

Quick answers

Can you leave a turkey in cold water overnight?

No. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. An overnight soak breaks both rules.

How long does cold water thawing take?

30 minutes per pound. A 12 lb turkey takes 6 hours, and a 24 lb turkey takes 12 hours.

What should I do the night before instead?

Move the turkey to the fridge. Keep the fridge at 40 F or below, and the bird thaws overnight at a safe cold. A thawed turkey then keeps 1 to 2 days in the fridge.
