Why this date

Exactly: 12 lb Tuesday, December 22. 14 lb and 16 lb Monday, December 21. 18 lb and 20 lb Sunday, December 20. 22 lb and 24 lb Saturday, December 19.

Why these dates

Fridge thawing takes about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds. Keep the fridge at 40 F or below. Count back from Friday and you land on the dates above. Start early: a thawed turkey keeps 1 to 2 days in the fridge. Start late, and a half-frozen bird roasts unevenly.

A fresh bird instead

Fresh: buy 1 to 2 days before cooking, on Wednesday, December 23 or Thursday, December 24. Keep it in the fridge at 40 F or below, and cook it on Friday.

Already past your date?

Use cold water today. Seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag. Submerge it in cold tap water. It takes about 30 minutes per pound. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. Full steps: thawing a turkey in cold water. No time? Cook it from frozen. USDA says it is safe, and it takes at least half again as long.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your oven manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
