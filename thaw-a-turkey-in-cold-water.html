Can you thaw a turkey in cold water?

Yes. It is one of the two safe methods, per USDA. Allow 30 minutes per pound. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook the turkey right after it thaws.

The steps

  1. Keep the turkey in its original wrapper. It must be leak-proof. Wrap it in a plastic bag if it might leak.
  2. Fill a sink or a big pot with cold tap water. Cover the turkey fully.
  3. Change the water every 30 minutes. This keeps the water cold.
  4. Cook the turkey right after it thaws. Do not put it back in the fridge to cook later.

Use cold water only. Warm or hot water puts the outside of the bird in the danger zone. And never thaw a turkey on the counter.
