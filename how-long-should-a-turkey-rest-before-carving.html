How long should a turkey rest before carving?

20 minutes. For quality, let the turkey stand for 20 minutes before carving to allow juices to set. The turkey will carve more easily.

Resting is for taste, not safety

Resting is for taste, not safety

Resting does not finish cooking the bird, and it does not replace the thermometer. No. A meat thermometer: 165 F in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost thigh, and the innermost wing. Color is not a reliable signal. Check before the rest starts, then let it stand. If stuffing came out with the bird, take it out during the rest.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your oven manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
