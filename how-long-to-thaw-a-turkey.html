How long to thaw a turkey

In the fridge: about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds. Keep the fridge at 40 F or below. A 14 lb turkey needs 4 days. A 20 lb turkey needs 5 days. In cold water: 30 minutes per pound.

How the safe ways work

Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service.

The safe ways

The fridge is hands-off. Put the turkey on a tray, breast up. Leave it in its wrapper. Give it the full time in the table.

Cold water is faster. Seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag. Submerge it in cold tap water. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. Full steps: thawing a turkey in cold water.

MicrowavePer your microwave manualNo waiting

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your microwave manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
