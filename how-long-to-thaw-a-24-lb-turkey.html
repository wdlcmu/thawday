How long to thaw a 24 lb turkey

6 days in the fridge. For a 5 PM Thanksgiving dinner on Thursday, November 26, 2026, move it to the fridge on Friday, November 20. In cold water: 12 hours.

In the fridge

Fridge thawing takes about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds. That is the USDA rule. Keep the fridge at 40 F or below. Put the turkey on a tray, breast up. Leave it in its wrapper. Give it the full 6 days.

Once thawed, it keeps 1 to 2 days in the fridge before you cook it. An early start is safe. A late start is the problem.

For Thanksgiving 2026

Dinner is Thursday, November 26, 2026. Your band is "21 to 24 lb." Every bird in that band shares this move date: Friday, November 20. All the dates by weight are on the Thanksgiving dates page.

These are the same dates the ThawDay calculator gives for a 5 PM Thanksgiving dinner. Dinner at a different hour? The move date stays the same. The calculator stamps your exact time.

In a hurry? Cold water

Cold water takes 12 hours for 24 lb. Seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag. Submerge it in cold tap water. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. Full steps: thawing a turkey in cold water.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your oven manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
