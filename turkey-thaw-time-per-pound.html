Turkey thaw time per pound

Fridge: 1 day per 4 to 5 lb. Cold water: 30 minutes per pound. Buy a 14 lb bird on Friday, November 20. Put it in the fridge by Sunday, November 22.

The fridge rule

USDA rate: 24 hours per 4 to 5 pounds. Keep the fridge at 40 F or below. Leave the bird in its wrap, on a tray.

The water rule

USDA rate: 30 minutes per pound. Seal the bird in a leak-proof bag. Change the water each 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws.

Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The skin warms while the core stays frozen. Germs grow fast in that warm zone. Cook a thawed bird in 1 to 2 days.
