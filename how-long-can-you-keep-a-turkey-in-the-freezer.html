How long can you keep a turkey in the freezer?

One year for best quality, and far longer for safety. Keep frozen until you're ready to thaw it. Turkeys can be kept frozen in the freezer indefinitely; however, cook within 1 year for best quality.

Safe to eat is not the same as still tasting good

Safe to eat is not the same as still tasting good

The 1 year line is about flavor and texture, not safety. A bird that stayed frozen does not turn unsafe at month thirteen; it can dry out and lose flavor. Keep the freezer at 0 F or below. A bird that thawed warm is different: can you refreeze a thawed turkey.

Quick answers

How long can you keep a turkey in the freezer?

Turkeys can be kept frozen in the freezer indefinitely; however, cook within 1 year for best quality.

Is a turkey still safe after a year in the freezer?

Yes, if it stayed frozen the whole time. The 1 year line is about quality, not safety, so cook within 1 year for best quality.

How long do turkey parts and giblets keep in the freezer?

For best quality, turkey parts keep up to 9 months in the freezer, and giblets 3 to 4 months. A whole bird keeps up to 1 year.
