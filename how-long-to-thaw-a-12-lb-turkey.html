How long to thaw a 12 lb turkey

3 days in the fridge. For a 5 PM Thanksgiving dinner on Thursday, November 26, 2026, move it to the fridge on Monday, November 23. In cold water: 6 hours.

Why this date

In the fridge

Fridge thawing takes about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds. Keep the fridge at 40 F or below. Put the turkey on a tray, breast up. Leave it in its wrapper. Your band is 8 to 12 lb; every bird in it shares this date.

Once thawed, cook it within 1 to 2 days in the fridge. Start early; a late start is the problem.

Cold water, step by step

In a hurry? Cold water

Cold water takes 6 hours for 12 lb. Seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag. Submerge it in cold tap water. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. Full steps: thawing a turkey in cold water.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your microwave manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
