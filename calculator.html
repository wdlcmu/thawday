FAQ

How much turkey per person?

Plan one pound of whole turkey per person. For leftovers, add half a pound per person. Bone and skin make up much of the weight.

Where do these numbers come from?

The USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service thaw chart and 325 F roasting chart. See footer date.

My turkey is still frozen and dinner is tomorrow. Am I doomed?

Use the cold-water method: seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag, submerge it in cold tap water, about 30 minutes per pound, change the water every 30 minutes, cook it right after it thaws.

Can I cook a turkey from frozen?

Yes. USDA says it is safe, and it takes at least 50 percent longer than a thawed bird. At 325 F, a 15 lb bird needs 5 hours 45 minutes to 6 hours 30 minutes from frozen. Do not use an oven bag. Pull the giblet packet out with tongs after 20 to 30 minutes of baking. The cook-from-frozen section on this page gives the time for your bird.

Stuffed or unstuffed?

This tool plans turkeys with no stuffing. For optimum safety, stuffing a turkey is not recommended. For more even cooking, it is recommended you cook your stuffing outside the bird in a casserole. The stuffing must reach a safe minimum internal temperature of 165 F.

How do I know it is done?

A meat thermometer: 165 F in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost thigh, and the innermost wing. Color is not a reliable signal.

Can I trust the pop-up timer?

If your turkey has a pop-up temperature indicator, it is recommended that you also check the internal temperature of the turkey in the innermost part of the thigh and wing and the thickest part of the breast with a food thermometer. The minimum internal temperature should reach 165 F for safety.

How long should the turkey rest before carving?

For quality, let the turkey stand for 20 minutes before carving to allow juices to set. The turkey will carve more easily.

Should I wash the turkey before cooking?

No. Washing or rinsing any meat or poultry is not recommended, and actually increases the risk you will cross-contaminate germs to other foods you're preparing.

How long do leftovers keep?

Use refrigerated turkey, stuffing, and gravy within 3 to 4 days. Discard any turkey, stuffing, and gravy left out at room temperature longer than 2 hours; 1 hour in temperatures above 90 F. Divide leftovers into smaller portions. Refrigerate or freeze in covered shallow containers for quicker cooling. If freezing leftovers, use within 2 to 6 months for best quality.

Does this work for Christmas dinner?

The math is the same. Change the dinner date to Friday, December 25, or see the Christmas move dates.

What if my turkey is over 24 pounds?

This tool covers 4 to 24 lb birds, the range in the USDA charts it uses. For a bigger bird, call the USDA Meat and Poultry Hotline at 1-888-674-6854. Real food safety experts answer on weekdays.
