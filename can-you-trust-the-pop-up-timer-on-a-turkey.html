Can you trust the pop-up timer on a turkey?

Not on its own. If your turkey has a pop-up temperature indicator, it is recommended that you also check the internal temperature of the turkey in the innermost part of the thigh and wing and the thickest part of the breast with a food thermometer. The minimum internal temperature should reach 165 F for safety.

What the timer actually reads

Pop-up timers are disposable thermometers used to measure a turkey’s temperature. These timers are a great tool. However, don’t forget to check in the three recommended places as well. It reads one spot in the bird, and the rest can lag behind that spot.

The check that decides

A meat thermometer: 165 F in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost thigh, and the innermost wing. Color is not a reliable signal. Turkey is safe to eat once it reaches an internal temperature of 165 F. If any one of the three reads under 165 F, the bird goes back in. When all three read 165 F, it rests 20 minutes before carving.
