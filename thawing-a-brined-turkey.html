How to thaw a turkey you plan to brine

Brining does not change the thaw. A frozen turkey thaws on the same days whether you brine it or not. The bird must be fully thawed when brining starts, 24 hours before the oven.

If the thaw runs late

If the thaw runs late

If it is still icy at brine time, use a shorter brine once it thaws, or skip the brine. A last-day cold-water rescue leaves no room for a full 24 hour brine.

Finishing a thaw in cold water: Seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag. Submerge it in cold tap water. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. Full steps: thawing a turkey in cold water.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your microwave manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
