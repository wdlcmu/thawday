Turkey still frozen on Thanksgiving morning?

Dinner can still happen. You have two safe paths: thaw it in cold water, at 30 minutes per pound, or cook it from frozen. Never the counter, and never warm water.

Path 1: cold water, if the hours fit

Cold water takes 30 minutes per pound. The bird must be thawed by the time it should go in the oven. For a 5 PM dinner today, Thursday, November 26, 2026, start by:

WeightCold water timeStart by
12 lb6 hoursThu, Nov 26, 7:47 AM
16 lb8 hoursThu, Nov 26, 4:40 AM
20 lb10 hoursThu, Nov 26, 2:17 AM
24 lb12 hoursWed, Nov 25, 11:55 PM

Seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag. Submerge it in cold tap water. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. Full steps: thawing a turkey in cold water.

Partly thawed already? These hours are for a rock frozen bird. Yours finishes sooner. Reading this at breakfast with a 20 lb bird? Cold water cannot finish in time. Path 2 is your dinner.

Path 2: cook it from frozen

USDA says cooking a frozen turkey is safe. Roast it at 325 F. It takes at least half again as long as a thawed bird.

WeightTime from frozen, at 325 F
12 lb4 hours 15 minutes to 4 hours 30 minutes
16 lb5 hours 45 minutes to 6 hours 30 minutes
20 lb6 hours 30 minutes to 6 hours 45 minutes
24 lb6 hours 45 minutes to 7 hours 30 minutes

Two rules that matter: no oven bag, and pull the giblet packet out with tongs once the outside softens. The thermometer decides: 165 F. Full steps: cooking a turkey from frozen.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your oven manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
