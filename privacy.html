Privacy

Privacy

The short version: Your dinner details stay in your browser. Cloudflare and PostHog count visits to this site. There are no accounts or sign-ups.

What the calculator does

You pick the date, time, and turkey weight.

You say frozen or fresh, and brine or not.

The timeline math runs in your browser.

Nothing you type is sent to us.

It saves no plan details on your device.

It saves a small flag instead.

The flag says a plan was made.

Sharing your plan

The share button copies your plan text.

You choose where to paste it.

The turkey card is drawn in your browser.

Card sharing uses your device share sheet.

Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Site counts

This page also loads Cloudflare Web Analytics.

It sends page and performance counts to Cloudflare.

Cloudflare says its beacon uses no cookies.

Cloudflare says its beacon does not read local storage.

The home page and calculator run PostHog.

PostHog counts page views and tool starts.

It counts plan finishes, shares, and downloads.

Your typed answers are never sent to it.

It does not record your screen.

Our code reads the flag for return visits.
