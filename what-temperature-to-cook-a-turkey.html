What temperature to cook a turkey?

325 F. That is the floor, not a suggestion. Set the oven temperature no lower than 325 F and be sure the turkey is completely thawed.

The pan the bird cooks in

Place turkey breast-side up on a flat wire rack in a shallow roasting pan 2 to 2-1/2 inches deep. The rack lifts the bird out of its own juices. The bottom then cooks at the same speed as the top. A deep pan traps steam around the lower half. That slows the bottom down.

The temperature that ends the roast

The dial starts the roast. The thermometer ends it. A meat thermometer: 165 F in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost thigh, and the innermost wing. Color is not a reliable signal. A pop-up timer does not replace that check: can you trust the pop-up timer on a turkey.

Lower and slower is not a shortcut

There is no low overnight roast in the plan. The USDA rule sets a floor under the oven. It does not just name a standard setting. If timing is the problem, the same 325 F covers the other path. A turkey can go in from frozen. It takes at least 50 percent longer. See can you cook a turkey from frozen.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your oven manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
