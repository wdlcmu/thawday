Can you thaw a turkey in the sink?

Not the way most people mean it. A turkey sitting in a sink of warm or standing water is not safe. The sink is fine as a tub for cold water thawing, done by the rules below.

Water cold onlyChange it every 30 min
What goes wrong

What goes wrong

Standing water warms to room temperature. The outside sits warm for hours while the inside stays frozen, and bacteria grow in that warm layer. Warm or hot water makes it worse.

The safe sink method

The safe sink method

The sink is just the tub for the USDA cold water method: 30 minutes per pound. Seal the bird in a leak-proof plastic bag. Submerge it in cold tap water. Change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws. Cover the bird fully; each change keeps it cold while it thaws.

The hands-off way: the fridge

The hands off way

No time to change water every 30 minutes? Use the fridge: days, but no watching. Dates by weight: how long to thaw a turkey.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your microwave manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
