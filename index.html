Common questions

When do I start thawing a frozen turkey?

In the fridge, allow 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds. A 12 to 16 pound turkey needs 3 to 4 days, so move it to the fridge 4 days before you cook to be safe. Once thawed, it keeps in the fridge for 1 to 2 days before cooking.

Is fridge thawing or cold water thawing better?

The fridge is the hands off option: 24 hours per 4 to 5 pounds, with nothing to watch. Cold water is faster, about 30 minutes per pound, but you must change the water every 30 minutes and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter.
