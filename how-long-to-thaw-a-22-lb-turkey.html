How long to thaw a 22 lb turkey

A 22 lb turkey needs about 6 days in the fridge. Move it on Friday, November 20. Dinner is Thursday, November 26. In cold water, it needs 11 hours.

In the fridge

Keep the fridge at 40 F or below. Leave the bird in its wrap, on a tray. USDA rate: 24 hours per 4 to 5 pounds. Start a big bird a full week out.

In cold water

Seal the bird in a leak-proof bag. Sink it in cold tap water. Change the water each 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws.

Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The skin warms while the core stays frozen. Germs grow fast in that warm zone. Cook a thawed bird in 1 to 2 days.
