Can you thaw a turkey in the microwave?

Yes. USDA counts the microwave as a safe way to thaw. Follow your oven manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. This page prints no microwave minutes, on purpose: your manual is the only honest source for them.

How microwave thawing works

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your oven manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. The manual knows your oven's power and the size of bird it can hold. A whole turkey is big, so many home microwaves cannot fit one, or cannot turn it. That is a manual question, not a chart question.

Two rules hold for every microwave thaw. Cook it right after it thaws, with no fridge pause in between. And never thaw a turkey on the counter while you wait: the outside warms up hours before the inside thaws.

If the bird is too big for the microwave

Cold water is the fast full size path: 30 minutes per pound. The fridge is the slow hands off path: about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds, at 40 F or below.
