Can you cook a turkey from frozen?

Yes. USDA says it is safe. Roast it at 325 F. It takes at least half again as long as a thawed bird. A 15 lb turkey needs about 5 hours 45 minutes to 6 hours 30 minutes.

The two rules that matter

Do not use an oven bag. It cannot take the long roast.

Pull the giblet packet out with tongs. Do it 20 to 30 minutes in, once the outside softens. The neck may need longer. Leave both out of the bird.

The chart aims, the thermometer decides: 165 F. Check the thickest part of the breast, the innermost thigh, and the innermost wing. Color is not a reliable signal.
