Why these dates

Fridge thawing takes about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds. That is the USDA rule. Keep the fridge at 40 F or below. Count back from Thursday and you land on the dates above.

An early start is safe. A thawed turkey keeps 1 to 2 days in the fridge. A late start is the problem: a half-frozen bird roasts unevenly.

Already past your date?

Use cold water today. Seal the turkey in a leak-proof plastic bag and submerge it in cold tap water. It takes about 30 minutes per pound. Change the water every 30 minutes, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Full steps: thawing a turkey in cold water.

No time at all? You can cook a turkey from frozen. USDA says it is safe. It takes at least half again as long.

Never thaw a turkey on the counter. Use the fridge or cold water. Microwave thawing is USDA-safe too: follow your oven manual, and cook the turkey right after it thaws.
