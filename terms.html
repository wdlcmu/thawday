What ThawDay is

These terms cover this website only.

ThawDay is a free turkey planning tool.

Tell it when dinner is.

It plans buy, thaw, brine, roast times.

This version plans unstuffed birds only.

It covers turkeys from 8 to 24 pounds.

There are no ads on this site.

There are no accounts or sign-ups.

Where times come from

Thaw times come from USDA charts.

Roasts use the USDA 325 F chart.

Some steps use kitchen practice instead.

All times are planning estimates only.

Your turkey may need more time.

Your turkey may need less time.

Food safety

Never thaw a turkey on the counter.

Use a meat thermometer when in doubt.

The bird must reach 165 F.

Check the thickest part of the breast.

Check the innermost thigh and wing.

Color is not a reliable signal.

Change cold water every 30 minutes.

Cook right after cold water thawing.

Follow official guidance if sources disagree.

You are responsible for safe food handling.
