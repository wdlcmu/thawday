How long do turkey leftovers last?

3 to 4 days in the fridge. Use refrigerated turkey, stuffing, and gravy within 3 to 4 days. For Thanksgiving 2026, dinner is Thursday, November 26, so the window ends Sunday, November 29 to Monday, November 30.

Throw out food left out over 2 hours

Throw out food left out over 2 hours

Discard any turkey, stuffing, and gravy left out at room temperature longer than 2 hours; 1 hour in temperatures above 90 F.

Thanksgiving breaks this rule most often: a long meal still counts by the clock. Food left out past the line goes out, even if it looks and smells fine.

Storing them so the days count

How to store them so they last 3 to 4 days

Divide leftovers into smaller portions. Refrigerate or freeze in covered shallow containers for quicker cooling. Shallow containers chill fast and evenly. Big, deep tubs cool slowly in the middle.

Freezing buys months, not days. If freezing leftovers, use within 2 to 6 months for best quality. A raw, thawed bird is different: it keeps only 1 to 2 days; see how long a thawed turkey stays in the fridge.

USDA's third safe way is the microwave: follow your microwave manual for size and timing, and cook the turkey right after it thaws. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. The outside warms up hours before the inside thaws. Bacteria grow in that warm layer.
