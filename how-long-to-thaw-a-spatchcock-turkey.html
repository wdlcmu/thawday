How long to thaw a spatchcock turkey

Thaw it whole, before you flatten it — the times are the same as any turkey. About 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds in the fridge. A 14 lb bird needs 4 days. For Thanksgiving 2026, move it on Sunday, November 22. In cold water: 30 minutes per pound. You cut the backbone out after the thaw, never before.

Then flatten it

Thaw first, then spatchcock

One: thaw the bird whole, in the fridge or in cold water, using the dates above. Two: with the breast down, cut along both sides of the backbone and remove it. Three: flip the bird and press down hard on the breastbone until it lies flat. Four: roast it flat at 325 F. A frozen or half-frozen bird cannot be cut or pressed flat safely, which is why the thaw comes first.

Brining or dry-brining? Do it after the thaw, while the bird is still whole or right after flattening: how long to brine a turkey.

How long does it cook?

Flat cooks faster — the thermometer decides

A flattened bird roasts faster and more evenly than a whole bird. There is no separate spatchcock line in the USDA chart this site uses, so we do not print one. Use the whole-bird chart below as the longest your bird can take, start checking well before it, and let the thermometer call it done: 165 F in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost thigh, and the innermost wing.
