Thaw a turkey fast

Cold water is the fast, safe way. It thaws 1 lb in 30 minutes. Found a frozen 14 lb bird on Wednesday, November 25? Start the water that day. It thaws in 7 hours, in time for Thursday.

Cold water, step by step

Seal the bird in a leak-proof bag. Sink it in cold tap water. Change the water each 30 minutes. Cook it right after it thaws.

No time at all?

You can cook a frozen bird. USDA says it is safe. It takes about half as long again to roast. See cooking a turkey from frozen.

Never use hot water. Never thaw on the counter. The skin warms while the core stays frozen. Germs grow fast in that warm zone.
